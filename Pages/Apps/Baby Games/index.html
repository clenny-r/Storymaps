Baby Games

Gentle, bright little games for small hands — and a kind voice to go with them.

✈️ Plane mode

Save every game, photo and voice clip on this phone once, on Wi-Fi. After that the games work in Airplane Mode, with no internet at all.

For the grown-up 👋

  • 🗣️Grace's voice is built in. Every word in the games is recorded ahead of time, so she answers straight away with nothing to download. Tap 🗣️ Voice if you would rather use the phone's own voice.
  • ⛶Full screen and Voice live at the top of every game, never at the bottom where Safari's toolbar hides them. On an iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen gives a true full screen with no address bar at all.
  • 🌙Night mode is the moon button, top right. It dims every game for bedtime and is remembered across all of them.
  • 🔒Stop little fingers escaping: iPhone → Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access. Android → screen pinning.
