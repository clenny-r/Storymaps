Plain-language glossary

AIP (Atlantic Immigration Program): PR route for workers with an endorsed job offer from an Atlantic employer. CBCL is the employer.
CEC (Canadian Experience Class): PR route for people with at least a year of skilled work in Canada, selected through Express Entry.
Express Entry draw and cut-off: IRCC invites profiles above a score cut-off every few weeks. Last CEC cut-off: 519.
CELPIP: the approved English test. ECA: official check that foreign degrees match Canadian ones.
Right of PR fee: $600 per adult, paid once when PR is approved.
