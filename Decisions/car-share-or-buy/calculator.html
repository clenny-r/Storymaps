What you need to know

  • Today: about 45 Communauto trips a year (about 3 hours and 22 km each). Car sharing costs about $58 per pay; owning a used car about $308 per pay (with a loan).
  • The deadline: plan changes start on the 1st of a month. To move to Value Plus from December 1, request it by November 30. Value Plus saves about $110 a year at today's usage; each plan change restarts a 12-month commitment.
  • What tips the scale: school runs alone do not. With school runs, owning becomes cheaper once Shadene drives to work 5 days a week with a car loan, or 4 days if you pay cash.
  • If you buy: 2020 to 2021 Kia Forte in Halifax lists for $14,000 to $19,000; add 14% HST on private sales. Get a real insurance quote and a loan pre-approval first; both are estimates here.
  • The $500 Communauto bond is refundable once you leave, after December 10, 2026 and 3 months' written notice.

Where the numbers come from
  • Errand trips: your 41 real trips (about 3 hours, 22 km each). Average before tax $18.19 on Value Extra, $20.72 on Value Plus.
  • School runs and commutes use Halifax weekday rates: Value Plus $3.65/hour (max $29/day) + 43¢/km; Value Extra $3.35/hour (max $26/day) + 34¢/km, or the $25 Workday rate (up to 10 hours, 40 km included, then 39¢/km).
  • The calculator picks the cheaper of Value Plus and Value Extra, and adds $0-deductible damage protection ($19.50/month).
  • Loan: standard monthly payment on price plus 14% HST minus your down payment. The yearly cost counts the average yearly loan interest plus 3% lost interest on your down payment. Default 10% APR (annual percentage rate): Canadian car loans are roughly 7% to 12% with good credit (loanscanada.ca); a short Canadian credit history can mean the higher end. Get a real pre-approval.
  • Owning: 11% yearly depreciation, $800 maintenance, $200 winter tires, $118 registration and inspection, 7 L/100 km, 5¢/km extra wear.
  • Your $500 Communauto bond is refundable, so it is not counted as a cost.
