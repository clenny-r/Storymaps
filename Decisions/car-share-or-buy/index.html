Plain-language glossary

Value Plus / Value Extra: Communauto plans. A higher monthly fee buys cheaper hourly and per-km rates. Value Extra is $30 a month; Value Plus is $12.50.
Workday rate: a Value Extra perk: $25 for up to 10 hours on a weekday, 40 km included. Useful for a daily car commute.
Depreciation: the value a car loses each year just by getting older. We never see it as a bill, but it is lost when we sell.
APR (annual percentage rate): the yearly interest rate on a car loan. Canadian used-car loans run roughly 7% to 12% with good credit.
Bond: the $500 deposit Communauto holds. It is not a cost; it comes back when we leave, after one year and 3 months' notice.
Tipping point: how much driving makes owning cheaper. For us it is Shadene driving to work 5 days a week (with school runs), not school runs alone.

Decision

Recommendation: keep Communauto and move to Value Plus from 1 Dec 2026. At our current driving, car sharing costs about $250 per pay less than owning.
When to revisit: if car school runs start, Value Extra becomes slightly cheaper. If Shadene will drive to work most days, buying a used car wins: give Communauto 3 months' notice so the bond comes back around the time we buy.
