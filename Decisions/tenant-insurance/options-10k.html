Reading it

Cheapest safe option at $10k: Westland with extended water at $11.36 per pay. Below that we are betting the building never floods.
Best value overall: TD at $12.84 per pay is only $1.48 more per pay than the best $10k option, and covers $20,000 more belongings plus six weeks of hotel instead of one.
Nothing reaches $10 a month ($4.62 per pay) at this address; $8.38 per pay is the floor and it leaves out the most likely Halifax claim.
