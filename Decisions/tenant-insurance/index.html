Plain-language glossary

Deductible: the first slice of any claim we pay ourselves. $1,000 deductible on a $6,000 loss means we pay $1,000 and the insurer pays $5,000. It applies per claim.
Sewer backup: water or sewage coming back up drains and toilets when the city sewer or building drains are overwhelmed in heavy rain.
Extended (overland) water: rain, snowmelt or street flooding coming in from outside. Standard policies only cover sudden internal water like a burst pipe.
Additional living expenses: hotel, meals and storage if the apartment becomes unlivable after a covered event.

Decision

Recommendation: TD at $12.84 per pay. It is the cheapest at full cover, gives six weeks of hotel instead of one, and gets cheaper if we ever add a car.
If we want the bare minimum: see the $10,000 options. The floor is $8.38 per pay with no water cover; $11.36 per pay with it. Only $1.48 per pay separates the cheapest safe option from TD's full cover.
